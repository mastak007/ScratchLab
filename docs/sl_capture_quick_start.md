# SL Capture — professional DJ quick start

SL CAPTURE — PRO DJ HANDOFF — 4 OCTOBER 2026

WHAT THIS IS
The full SL Capture Mac authoring app, version 1.0.1 (24), with generic performer wording
and explicit DJ attribution. Supports Intel and Apple silicon; requires macOS 15 or later.
This is a supervised capture candidate. It is not certification of every rig.

INSTALL
1. Copy this folder from the ZIP to the Mac. Quit any running SL Capture.
2. Keep any previous app and all recordings. Copy SL Capture.app to Applications.
   If an app with that name exists, retain it before replacing; don't delete data.
3. Open SL Capture. Complete its consent/setup screens truthfully and allow the
   camera/microphone permissions required for the chosen recording setup.
4. This package has a valid Developer ID signature but is NOT notarized.
   If macOS says Apple cannot check it, after the opening attempt use System
   Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway for this specific trusted app.
   Follow Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-nz/102445
   Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine in Terminal. If it reports
   damage/malware or no exception is allowed, retain the exact message and stop.
   A managed Mac may require its administrator. First launch on this Mac is
   still untested; this package does not guarantee it can run there.

FIRST FIVE MINUTES — PROVE THE NEW RIG BEFORE RECORDING THE TEACHING SET
1. Connect the actual controller/audio interface and camera. In Setup choose the
   real MIDI source, camera and audio input. Enable Selected Camera & Audio.
2. Frame both hands, platter and fader. Leave optional Watch relay off unless
   deliberately testing it. Use the correct deck and hardware-specific mapping;
   a Rane ONE MKII acceptance does not certify a Seventy-Two/Twelve or another rig.
3. Load AHHH and choose the intended AHHH output. Verify the sound in the actual
   headphones/speakers and verify each fader cut. Check live movement/control
   evidence. Do not transfer Karl's machine-specific saved equipment settings.
4. Select Movement check (no beat), apply setup, record a short movement check,
   then Stop and Finalize. Play whole take: check saved sound and picture.
5. Save Capture to a ZIP, then reopen the saved draft for review. Confirm the
   saved take and exported files before spending time on the teaching session.
   A movement check is diagnostic and cannot be approved as a timed reference.

RECORD TEACHING TAKES
Use New scratch > Reference take (four repetitions). Enter the real DJ / performer
name in Setup. Use New scratch before changing performers. Enter the actual
technique/variation, BPM, phrase, handedness and notes; Apply Authoring Setup.
Record Draft and perform the four timed slots through the automatic finish.
Stopping early produces diagnostic evidence, not a complete reference take.
Review picture and sound, mark the preferred repetition and add expert notes.
Save Capture even if canonical approval is blocked; preserve original media.
Each reference-authoring Save Capture ZIP includes PNG references in notation/:
take-001_detected_notation_reference_001.png (and further numbered pages for longer takes).
Open the ZIP to view them. Four seconds per row, up to four rows per image;
unknown motion and separate fader evidence stay visible. Existing saved drafts
can be opened for review and saved again to generate the PNGs without recording.
The images are visual references; the JSON and original media remain the evidence.
Saved drafts reopen on the recording Mac. A raw ZIP is not an importable draft.
If evidence supports approval, Export Approved Package produces a separate
reference package; use Reopen & Verify Last Export. Keep both export types.
Do not force approval or relabel unknown motion as stillness to make it pass.

WHAT TO BRING BACK
Raw Save Capture ZIPs, any approved reference packages, each DJ's technique/cue
notes, agreed teaching/distribution permissions and a brief setup/check result.
Keep a second copy of originals on another drive before leaving.
Read CAPTURE BRIEF.txt for the smallest useful teaching set.

LIMITS
Previous bounded audio/fader/render/save/export evidence applies to Karl's rig.
Calibration, external mixer master-return audio and the DJ's rig remain unverified.
The app's generated scratch audio is not proof of the physical mixer master.
No user recordings, equipment profiles, credentials or archival video library
are included. Existing signing identity, entitlements and saved recordings are preserved.

PRO DJ TEACHING SESSION — PROPOSED BRIEF

Goal: establish one reliable Baby Scratch teaching example first, then expand to
three agreed foundational skills, reviewed with the three professional DJs.
Baby Scratch, plain Tear and Chirp are the current candidates, not a decision already approved by the reviewing DJ.

Before recording, agree for each selected skill:
- One visible beginner goal and the exact variation/phrase.
- A short explanation of the hand mechanics and one common error/correction.
- What the learner can self-review; what evidence would support automatic feedback.
- Permission to use the chosen performance, voice, image, sample and beat in the
  free Learner, including distribution/editing. Model training is a separate use.

Aim for one usable slow and one normal-tempo take per agreed skill, reusing good
existing recordings if suitable. Perform at the stated tempo. Keep explanations
separate from clean performance audio. Frame both hands, platter and fader.
Record the exact BPM, performer, handedness, technique/variation and setup.
Use four repetitions for a reference take and let the performing DJ choose the preferred one.

Quality means clear teaching and intact, correctly identified evidence. Do not
chase cosmetic perfection or remove uncertainty by inventing motion/fader data.
If rapid short strokes are rejected as unknown, retain raw evidence, timestamps
and the DJ's explanation of the intended mechanics. That is useful evidence for a
bounded teaching-fitness investigation; do not silently loosen decoder rules.

Approved video/audio supports watch/listen instruction. It does not establish
physical platter scale or measured cut timing by itself. Automatic amplitude or
coordination scoring needs separately validated motion/fader data and a rubric.
Nothing in this brief authorizes model training or fabricating a measured target.

Before leaving: replay one saved example, reopen its draft, verify exported files,
copy exports and notes to Karl, and keep the original recordings plus a backup.
