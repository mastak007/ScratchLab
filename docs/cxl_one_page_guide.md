# ScratchLab CXL quick start

Four attempts of one variation in each take

Use the Mac to start, stop, review and save. Complete one short check on your own decks before recording a batch. iPhone video and Watch motion are optional.

Before you start

Devices Mac with macOS 15 or later; the ScratchLab phone app needs iOS 26.5 or later. Optional Apple Watch needs watchOS 10 or later and must be paired with that iPhone. Confirm your devices meet these versions before installing.

Accounts and connections For an iPhone camera, sign the Mac and iPhone into your own same Apple Account with two-factor authentication. Keep Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, devices nearby and on the same local network. You do not need Karl’s account. Use power and secure camera mounts; allow Camera, Microphone and Local Network when requested.

Install Both apps come through Apple’s TestFlight app. On the Mac, install TestFlight from the Mac App Store, accept Karl’s ScratchLab CXL invitation and install it. On iPhone, accept the ScratchLab invitation and install it; in its TestFlight details install the Watch app, then open ScratchLab on the Watch. Keep both betas updated; builds expire after 90 days.

Set up the picture and sound

1  Select the hardware Open ScratchLab CXL → Setup. Choose the actual MIDI controller, audio input and main camera; press Enable Selected Camera & Audio. With Seventy-Two + Twelve, select Seventy-Two as the main MIDI source and Twelve under Separate Twelve platter, physically set to Deck 2. Frame both hands, platter and crossfader from about 45° in landscape. Learn and calibrate the fader for your active deck and open end; check the live fader state.

2  Check what you hear Use Scratch sample → Load / Re-cue (AHHH, Fresh, Ah yeah or Check it out), select the intended output, then Preview backing sound. Confirm BOTH scratch and beat in the actual headphones. Rane ONE/MKII uses right USB 3/4 for scratch and left USB 1/2 for beat; cue both decks. For Seventy-Two, choose Scratch USB outputs and Beat USB outputs in Mixer & Hot-Cue Mapping, set the mixer decks to the Mac’s USB A/B connection, then test each pair. Use Learn for its faders and hot cues; assign samples and choose a fader curve there. These choices are saved for that device.

3  Optional second view or Watch For an iPhone view, enable Continuity Camera in iPhone Settings → General → AirPlay & Continuity. Select it under Second camera, mount it in portrait showing your upper body and decks, and choose Portrait 90° or 270° until upright. Check both previews. Camera choices can be changed between takes.

For Watch motion, on iPhone open ScratchLab → Advanced / Mac Companion → Watch Capture, tap Connect Mac if offered, and keep it open. Enable iPhone & Watch Relay on the Mac. If searching persists, use Reconnect Mac on iPhone and Reconnect iPhone relay on Mac. A Watch paired to the phone does not prove a Mac connection. Do not rely on the same phone for simultaneous Continuity Camera and uninterrupted Watch relay; use another camera or leave Watch capture off.

Record and send

4  Make a short check Choose Movement check before Apply Authoring Setup. Record a short movement, Stop and Finalize, then Play whole take and Save Capture. Verify sound, video and the second view if enabled. If anything fails, stop the batch and send Karl the exact message and exported ZIP.

5  Record four attempts Choose New scratch → Reference take. Choose technique, variation, BPM, direction, handedness and fader variant. For four beats scratching followed by four beats rest, set Phrase length to 2 bars. Apply Authoring Setup, press Record Draft, follow the count-in and repeat that same eight-beat phrase four times. Let the app finish. Keep talking out of the performance.

6  Choose the best and save Use Review bounds and Play repetition to watch and hear each attempt. Adjust Start/End to the actual scratch, then Mark as Preferred on the best one; add useful notes. If approval checks pass, use Approve & Save Capture. Review can wait: Save Capture works without approval. Send Karl the ZIP and retain your local drafts and originals. Use New scratch for a different variation.

Apple instructions: [Continuity Camera](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102546) · [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/).
