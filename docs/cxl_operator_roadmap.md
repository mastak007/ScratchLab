# CXL operator release roadmap

Status date: 8 September 2026, Pacific/Auckland. Integration candidate: `/private/tmp/scratchlab-cxl-operator-20260908.HS06xS/candidate`, branch `codex/cxl-operator-release`, based on the completed uncommitted TTM prompts 9–12 patch over `3f43345a6c6369a4b18bbb52134a1666ac505b30`.

The near-term target is a **CXL Capture Ready internal Release within four working days**, not completion of the public-beta curriculum. The build opens into **Session Setup → Capture → Review & Export**. Practice, coaching scores, learner progression, lesson browsing and unrelated experimental windows are excluded from its launch and primary navigation. Shared models, the serial authoring owner, capture services, persistence, export and the shared notation renderer remain authoritative.

Capture Ready means the supported CXL rig can launch the actual Release build; bind an exact technique/variant/beat version; perform Setup, Record, Stop, finalized media review, approve/reject and Next take; resolve or visibly block on Watch/source evidence; and save a truthful package that reopens with matching identity and hashes. It does not mean the 40-variant library, 160 accepted takes, learner curriculum or public beta is complete.

## Delivery order

1. **Next take progression.** Preserve an approved take and explicitly return the same session owner to ready. Do not auto-record, allocate a take, revalidate history, publish or train.
2. **Immutable capture intent.** Carry stable parent, variant and recipe/version identity, direction, fader form, BPM and beats/cycle through capture, review, export and reopen. Legacy absence stays unspecified.
3. **Three-day versioned CXL Beat Pilot.** Define immutable `BeatSpec` identity and version, BPM, time signature, feel, count-in, loop length, stem and master hashes, mix role and rights status. Deliver exactly six original, rights-clear variants: boom-bap straight at 80 BPM, boom-bap swing at 90 BPM, funk-break straight at 90 BPM, funk light-swing at 100 BPM, electro straight at 110 BPM and half-time at 80 BPM. Each variant includes one production master, one sample-aligned sparse analysis mix and its available original stems. No reference take may infer or silently substitute its beat. Additional families, feels and tempo renders stay outside this three-day gate.
4. **Recorded timing.** Establish count-in/media-start origin, supported beats/cycle and planned duration against measured artifacts. Nominal bars and BPM alone are not witnessed pacing. Verify every beat master and analysis-safe counterpart has sample-accurate matching loop/count-in boundaries.
5. **Finalized media review.** Audition the actual WAV/MOV take and selected range with explicit missing/synchronization states. Review against both the bound production beat and analysis-safe mix where available.
6. **Terminal Watch/source binding.** Resolve late Watch linkage and frozen sidecar continuity without silent rehashing, evidence omission or review loss.
7. **Approved package and reopen.** Connect the existing approved-package writer/reader/verifier to an operator export and another-installation verification path. Raw Save Capture remains diagnostic.
8. **Figma operator flow and organisation.** Inspect the actual existing ScratchLab Figma file with the required skills. Audit pages, sections, frames and prototype links; correct accidental overlaps and organisation while preserving components, learner designs and uncertain legacy material. Add clearly labelled CXL Operator Release wireframes for Setup, Capture and Review & Export at normal/small macOS sizes and real companion roles. Map proposed states to implemented contracts.
9. **CXL-only Release surface.** Implement the approved handoff, then verify actual Release launch, restoration, menu/window routes, permissions, identity and supported device roles.
10. **Authorized physical pilot.** On a supported rig, verify recording, timing, beat playback, Watch/source continuity, rejection/retake, recovery, package reopen and independent backup. Software gates do not establish this acceptance.

## Milestones

- **A — operator pilot:** dependencies 1–10, with Baby plus a plain-Tear hold and an independent fader-cut example. At least one sparse analysis beat and one production-ready beat must be bound and verified.
- **B0 — content rehearsal:** one complete Baby lesson and one coordination-heavy lesson through clean source, package/backup, separate explanation, original notation, captions and Release playback.
- **B — ordered production:** all 32 retained queue rows and at least 40 variants, with two accepted slow and two accepted demonstration takes per variant. Beat assignments cover the approved families and feels without claiming an exhaustive technique-by-beat cross-product.
- **C — public beta:** every offered variant has working Learn/listen, capture, actual review and save/export behavior, approved content, rights and distributed Release evidence.

## Three-day beat-pilot schedule

- **Day 1 — specification and source lock:** approve the six `BeatSpec` records, count-in/loop contract, loudness and headroom targets, original sound sources and rights receipts; program the core drums and bass parts.
- **Day 2 — production:** finish arrangements, production masters, sparse analysis mixes and stems; render only the six approved variants.
- **Day 3 — QA and handoff:** verify sample-aligned loops/count-ins, hashes, peaks/loudness, scratch audibility and rights records; complete CXL listening review and package the accepted assets. Failed variants remain failed or are replaced within the six-variant cap.

The three-day result is the pilot pack and its evidence, not the broader library. Seventy, 120 and 130 BPM renders, full-performance arrangements, extra families and exhaustive technique coverage remain later backlog items.

## Four-day Capture Ready schedule

Beat production runs in parallel on days 1–3. App work stays on the smallest existing shared paths and adds no alternate capture, review, renderer or export system.

- **Day 1 — close the recording loop:** finish and verify explicit Next take; add the smallest immutable capture-intent and `BeatSpec` binding needed to carry technique, variant/version, BPM, direction, fader form and exact beat version/hash through the current session and take. Lock the six-beat source plan.
- **Day 2 — truthful review:** witness count-in/media origin and supported duration from the finalized take; expose actual finalized audio/video playback with explicit missing and synchronization states. Continue beat production and sparse mixes.
- **Day 3 — durable output and Release route:** connect the existing approved-package writer/reader/verifier to the operator flow; preserve terminal Watch/source state and review history; implement the minimal direct CXL Release launch/navigation. Complete beat mastering, hashes, rights records and focused Setup/Capture/Review handoff frames.
- **Day 4 — acceptance and freeze:** run focused and full software gates, build the actual Release candidate, then perform one authorized supported-rig pilot covering Setup → Capture → Review → approve/reject → Next take → package/reopen/recovery. Freeze evidence and blockers. Capture Ready is claimed only if every mandatory gate passes.

### Four-day scope cuts

Deferred beyond Capture Ready: the 32-row/40-variant/160-take production run; public-beta lessons and narration; learner Practice/coaching/progression; full Figma-file reorganisation; additional beat families and 70/120/130 BPM renders; multi-rig and cross-platform polish; automatic publication, installation or training eligibility; and nonessential visual refinement. Existing unsupported or incomplete routes remain absent from the CXL Release navigation.

The four-working-day window targets only the evidence-defined CXL Capture Ready internal Release. It is not a promise to finish all 40 variants, 160 accepted takes or public-beta content. Commit, push, upload, publication, training, recording, hardware and distribution remain separately authorized actions.
